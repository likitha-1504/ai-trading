# Phase 27: Strategy Research & Optimization Framework

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

Phase 27 (**Strategy Research & Optimization**) introduces a rigorous, quantitative research framework that balances rapid algorithmic exploration with inviolable capital preservation and governance controls. It enables parameter tuning, indicator feature exploration, and hypothesis testing while enforcing four structural safeguards:
1. **Isolated Research Branch**: All research activities, backtests, and candidate models remain strictly isolated in branch `research/phase27-strategy-optimization`.
2. **Immutable Version Lineage**: Prior versions (notably `v1.0.0-baseline`) are preserved immutably; no overwriting of historical benchmarks.
3. **Data Leakage & Look-Ahead Auditing**: Every experimental pipeline is verified for causal monotonic ordering, temporal split boundary isolation, and zero look-ahead bias.
4. **Prohibition of Silent Production Entry**: No candidate strategy can enter production or replace the baseline without explicit operator authorization tokens (`OPERATOR-PHASE27-*`).

---

## 2. Inviolable Governance Principles

During Phase 27 research and future strategy evolution cycles:
- **Capital Caps**: The capital ceiling remains locked at **INR 100.00**, with the autonomous ceiling at **INR 300.00**.
- **Risk Invariants**: Maximum risk per trade remains capped at **1.5%**; daily maximum account drawdown circuit breaker remains at **3.0%**; 15:15 IST square-off remains deterministic.
- **Safety Mode**: `IS_PAPER_TRADING=True` is permanently active.
- **Production Isolation**: All changes are committed to the research branch. Merging into production requires a formal checkpoint sign-off and explicit operator review.

## 4. Leakage & Look-Ahead Bias Auditing

To eliminate historical simulation artifacts and statistical illusions, the `LeakageAuditor` validates four invariant properties:

1. **Monotonic Event Sequencing**:
   $$\forall i > 0, \quad T_{\text{event}, i} \ge T_{\text{event}, i-1}$$
   Any chronological inversion triggers an immediate `LeakageAuditReport(is_leakage_free=False)` and blocks evaluation.

2. **Temporal Split Boundary Isolation**:
   $$\max(T_{\text{train}}) < \min(T_{\text{test}})$$
   In-sample and out-of-sample datasets must have strict temporal separation. No rolling indicator warm-up or statistical aggregation is permitted to cross the partition boundary.

3. **Causal Signal Timestamping**:
   Signals generated by strategies must satisfy:
   $$T_{\text{signal}} \ge T_{\text{trigger\_event}}$$
   No signal may reference future bar High/Low/Close prices. Execution simulation uses the next available tick or quote price with realistic slippage.

## 5. Realistic Indian Cost & Slippage Simulation

All research backtests execute with realistic friction calculated by `IndianMarketCostModel`:
- **Brokerage**: 0.03% or INR 20 (whichever is lower).
- **STT**: 0.025% on sell-side equity turnover.
- **Exchange Turnover Charges**: 0.00297% (NSE).
- **SEBI Charges**: 0.0001% (INR 10 per crore).
- **Stamp Duty**: 0.003% on buy-side turnover.
- **GST**: 18.0% on (Brokerage + Exchange Charges + SEBI Charges).
- **Slippage**: Realistic 2.0 bps (1-2 ticks) simulation based on live market observations from Phase 26.

---

## 6. Walk-Forward Validation Engine

Strategies are evaluated using multi-window walk-forward cross-validation:
- **Windows**: 3 to 4 chronological rolling folds.
- **In-Sample Ratio**: 70% training / parameter tuning.
- **Out-of-Sample Ratio**: 30% blind out-of-sample forward testing.
- **Robustness Criterion**: Positive expectancy across 100% of out-of-sample folds and statistical stability score $\ge 0.85$.

---

## 7. Silent Production Entry Prevention Gate

To prevent untested code from reaching active production, the promotion path requires:
```python
registry.promote_experiment(
    experiment_id="EXP-STRAT-002",
    operator_authorization_token="OPERATOR-PHASE27-TOKEN-...",
    rationale="Validated candidate with +3.45 INR/trade expectancy.",
    target_environment="paper",
)
```
Any call with missing, invalid, or unapproved tokens raises `SilentProductionEntryError` (HTTP 403 Forbidden).
